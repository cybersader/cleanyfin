## Reading a sound-gated run

Use the same combined capture, consent and cleanup procedure. No new TV app, device permission, log level, or saved edited movie is needed. The 5.1 Dolby test file carries a position code; only Android's stereo Wholphin playback mix is judged. Never substitute a desktop decode, a player's timestamp, or microphone audio for the recorded mix.

- **FAIL** means a verified filtered take contained a marked position in at least one ear. A later capture failure cannot erase it. Counts are overlapping windows, not distinct leaks.
- **PASS for a take** requires two complete, independently mapped uncut controls on that route, a known marked positive and kept negative for this mark, a witnessed transfer and EOF, and every active block on the expected kept path. Even a recognizable kept block with unexplained energy above one captured s16 count makes clearance **unmeasured**. This is a recording-resolution result, not an audibility threshold.
- **Not measured** includes a 1 ms mark (below the witness floor), an unknown block, unverified start, incomplete sweep, or a pause/seek whose independent audio coverage cannot be established. Read `sound.blockOutcome` separately from `sound.outcome`: a recognized waveform does not clear a possible echo. The [detector limits](05-marked-detector.md#android-stereo-playback-mix) apply. R22 silence, pops, kept loss and A/V sync are separate tests.
- A **family safety rehearsal** plays no movie: `PASS family safety rehearsal` beside two `not-measured` material claims is the intended result. It cannot promote a tuple, and neither a sound nor a picture pass releases the household box.

Host audio cleanup now names the private null sink uniquely per run and checks that neither it nor its monitor exists on the desktop at teardown. Default sink/source changes still block rehearsal approval. Entire desktop inventories and anonymous churn counts are diagnostic only. The `tv-20261126T154535Z-da4c9449fa` receipt saved only booleans: both inventories changed, defaults stayed put, and the owned emulator and private namespace exited. ## Interrupted runs

Press Ctrl-C once and keep the TV, terminal and Wireless debugging on while the harness restores the **lab** settings, deletes its server and helper, removes its screenrecord and checks the updated app and original inventory. At the final prompt, forget only `cleanyfin@lab` under Paired devices, turn Wireless debugging off, and press Enter. The harness distinguishes a connection refusal from an inconclusive timeout. A hard kill, power loss or premature disconnect can prevent verification: do not rerun the update, clear data, uninstall Wholphin, downgrade it, or delete an unfamiliar entry. If present, remove only the printed `Cleanyfin Lab …` server via its stock long-press Delete menu and only the matching `CF Lab …` helper in Android Settings → Apps; reselect the original server/profile and sign in yourself if needed. If recording removal or settings restoration could not be verified, retain the receipt and obtain targeted cleanup rather than inferring a clean box.

## Measuring transport separately

The [pinned Wholphin intent handler](https://github.com/damontecres/Wholphin/blob/v1.1.2/app/src/main/java/com/github/damontecres/wholphin/services/IntentService.kt) reads the `position` long extra in milliseconds on its stock `PLAYBACK` intent. Its [server-event listener](https://github.com/damontecres/Wholphin/blob/v1.1.2/app/src/main/java/com/github/damontecres/wholphin/services/ServerEventListener.kt) advertises media control, but its shown subscriptions are general commands and user updates; capability flags do not prove a `PlayNow` or `Seek` was handled. Requested starts use the native intent; remote seeks still require distinct observed paused barcodes. Persisted resume requires a changed bookmark corresponding to a visibly stopped kept frame, a cold start and the stock Resume action. A single-mark private diagnostic records route counters, visible frames and available codec evidence without an admission claim.
